Community Guidelines

Last updated October 2, 2026

OUTHERENOW is for touching grass together. These guidelines keep it a good place to be.

Be kind and keep it real

Treat people the way you'd want to be treated at a jam. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, or discrimination of any kind.

Post real spots, real camps and honest reviews. No spam, ads, or fake listings.

Keep it safe

Don't share anyone's private information, including where someone lives.

No sexual content, nudity or graphic violence in photos, names, bios or descriptions.

Don't promote dangerous or illegal activity. Respect park rules, fire restrictions and the land.

If something's wrong

Use Report on the spot, camp, review, line post or profile, or long-press a person to report or block them. Blocking hides their posts from you right away.

We review every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these guidelines is removed, and accounts that post it are suspended or banned.

If someone is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.
